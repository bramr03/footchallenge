Why We're Doing This

Nowadays, many people struggle to stay active due to busy schedules or lack of motivation. Our event lowers the threshold for participation by offering a fun, accessible, and social sports experience.

At the same time, we contribute to one of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals — promoting well-being and inclusion for all. No prior experience needed. Just show up, kick the ball, and have a great time!

Activity Description

A football juggling challenge where participants try to keep the ball in the air using only their feet — for as long as possible!

How it works:
  1. Register at the welcome desk
  2. Receive a brief explanation of the rules
  3. Enter the juggling zone one by one
  4. Start juggling when the timer begins
  5. Keep the ball up using only your feet — no hands!
  6. Your best time is recorded on the scoreboard
🏆 Win a prize! The participant with the longest juggling time wins a small sports prize — like a reusable water bottle or a football.

Risk Management

⚠️ Potential Risks
  • Loss of balance or minor falls
  • Collision with other participants
  • Muscle strain or fatigue
  • Slipping on the surface
✅ Preventive Measures
  • Clear boundaries marked with cones
  • Only one participant at a time in the juggling zone
  • Mandatory sports-appropriate footwear
  • Short warm-up recommendation before participating
  • Staff supervision at all times
  • Adequate spacing between waiting and activity areas
🚑 Emergency Preparedness: First aid kit available on-site. Team members trained for minor injuries. Easy access to campus emergency services.
